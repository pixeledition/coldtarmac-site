Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Cold Tarmac is a private, single-operator content pipeline. This policy explains how the application handles data in relation to its TikTok API integration.

2. Data We Collect

This application does not collect, store, or process personal data from any third party. It operates as a private, single-user tool used exclusively by its owner.

3. TikTok API Usage

Cold Tarmac interfaces with the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload video content as drafts to the authenticated account owner's TikTok profile. The only data exchanged with TikTok is the video file, caption text, and cover image submitted by the pipeline operator.

4. No Third-Party Data Sharing

No personal data is shared with third parties. No analytics, advertising, or tracking mechanisms are used. No third-party users connect their TikTok accounts to this application.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies or any form of user tracking.

6. TikTok's Privacy Policy

Data handled on TikTok's platform is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

For any queries, contact the operator via the TikTok account @coldtarmac.
